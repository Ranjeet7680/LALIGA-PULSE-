2. Concept Summary: How LALIGA PULSE Works

LALIGA PULSE directly addresses all four opportunity areas specified in the challenge brief:

Layer 1: In-Match Participation (Synchronous Micro-Tactix)

Instead of slow weekly fantasy or predatory gambling, PULSE delivers 15-second synchronous tactical decision windows triggered dynamically when Mediacoach optical tracking detects critical phases (24m free-kick, 3v2 counter-attack breakaway, or high-press traps). Fans vote in real-time, with outcomes resolved in <2.4s by official TRACAB telemetry.

Layer 2: Structured Community (Global Peña Clan Wars)

Fans join or form verified digital supporter clubs (Peñas) such as Peña Madridista Tokyo, Cairo Culés, and Peña LALIGA Dubai. Individual tactical points pool into real-time matchday standings, creating tribal pride and collective belonging.

Layer 3: Data & Narrative Amplification (Beyond Stats 2.0)

PULSE translates Mediacoach tracking data into accessible consumer metrics: Momentum Shift Index (MSI™), Pressure Cooker Rating, and Crowd Tactical IQ (CTIQ™), giving broadcast commentators and fans instant storytelling hooks.

Layer 4: Extended Experience & FinTech Loyalty Loop

Engagement is sustained through mid-week tactical labs, in-stadium BLE beacon sync (synchronized phone flashlights and jumbotron voting), and a non-speculative loyalty wallet where fans earn $PULSE Credits redeemable for authentic club jerseys, LALIGA+ passes, and sponsor perks.

4. Ecosystem Integration Plan

LALIGA PULSE is architected as an expansion pack within LALIGA’s existing technological landscape, avoiding fragmented downloads or detached external portals.

Key Ecosystem Touchpoints:

  • Sportian / Mediacoach: Ingests TRACAB 25fps optical tracking and official event metadata via Apache Kafka & Azure Event Hubs streaming 150,000 coordinate points/min.
  • LALIGA Official App: Delivered as a high-performance modular React Native SDK / micro-frontend embedded natively in the flagship app.
  • LALIGA Fantasy Integration: Bi-directional reward loop: playing live PULSE duels unlocks exclusive bonus booster cards and XP inside LALIGA Fantasy.
  • Mediapro Broadcast & LALIGA+ OTT: Synchronous lower-third graphics generated via Broadcaster API; HLS ID3 timed metadata tags eliminate broadcast delay spoilers.

7. Risk Mitigation & Regulatory Compliance

100% Free-to-Play, Non-Gambling Compliance Architecture:
LALIGA PULSE requires no real-money deposits, enforces zero monetary cashouts, and operates strictly as a skill-based loyalty utility. Compliant with Spain DGOJ, UAE SCA & CBUAE, US State, and EU regulations without requiring gambling or gaming licenses.
Risk Vector Specific Vulnerability Architectural Mitigation
Broadcast Latency Variance OTT viewers watching 15s behind cable seeing spoilers. Adaptive Latency Buffer: Challenges calibrated to individual video timecodes via HLS ID3 tags or audio watermarking.
Data Protection & Privacy Cross-border storage of fan telemetry and geofencing. Privacy by Design: On-device geofencing validation without server GPS upload; pseudonymous tokens linked to LALIGA SSO (GDPR & UAE PDPL compliant).
Brand Safety & Toxicity Rival fanbases abusing live match chat rooms. AI NLP Toxicity Shield: Multilingual automated filtering (Arabic, Spanish, Catalan, English) with automatic chat cooldowns and clan reputation penalties.
